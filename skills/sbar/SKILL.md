# SBAR

Write the four sections in order, for a reader who has seen none of your session.

- **Situation.** Say in one or two sentences what is happening now, and what the reader must do and by when.
- **Background.** Give the facts the reader can't find alone: the goal or problem, the owner's constraints word for word, and where the work lives, which covers the machine, the folder, the branch, uncommitted files and running processes.
- **Assessment.** Give your judgment: the likely cause or state, how sure you are and why, what you ruled out, and what you did not check. Keep what you saw apart from what you infer.
- **Recommendation.** Name the first action to take, then the rest in order.
