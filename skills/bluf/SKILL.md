# Bottom line up front

State what the reader must do or decide, and by when, in the first sentence.
Then give the support.

- **Bottom line.** If the reader has nothing to do or decide, state the result. Progress is support, even when most of the work is done.
- **First sentence test.** A reader who stops after the first sentence can still act.
